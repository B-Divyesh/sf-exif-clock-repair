Privacy

Exif Clock Repair is designed to run locally in your browser. It does not upload photo files, EXIF metadata, filenames, or repair plans to us. It uses no advertising, analytics, tracking pixels, or third-party runtime scripts.

Local storage

To make a repair session survive a refresh, the app saves the current findings and selected proposed repairs in this browser’s local storage. Photo bytes are never stored. Use “Clear local plan” on every screen size to remove the plan, or clear the site’s browser data.

If you restore or purchase an Archive Support license, its token and last verification result are also stored locally. Only then does the app send that token to the Sociobot license API, at most once per day. It sends no photo or repair data.

Downloads

Ledgers and the bundled XMP sidecars are generated and downloaded directly by your browser. They do not pass through a server operated by this product.

Contact

This static product has no accounts and no contact form. If the product later adds a support address, this policy will be updated before collecting messages.
