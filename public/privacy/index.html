Privacy

Exif Clock Repair is designed to run locally in your browser. It does not upload photo files, EXIF metadata, filenames, or repair plans to us. It uses no advertising, analytics, tracking pixels, or third-party runtime scripts.

Local storage

To make a repair session survive a refresh, the app saves the current findings and selected proposed repairs in this browser’s local storage. Photo bytes are never stored. Use “Clear local plan” to remove it, or clear the site’s browser data.

Downloads

Ledgers and XMP sidecars are generated and downloaded directly by your browser. They do not pass through a server operated by this product.

Contact

This static product has no accounts and no contact form. If the product later adds a support address, this policy will be updated before collecting messages.
