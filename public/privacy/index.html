Privacy

Exif Clock Repair runs in your browser. It does not upload photo files, EXIF metadata, filenames, or repair plans. It has no advertising, analytics, tracking pixels, or third-party runtime scripts.

Local storage

To make a repair session survive a refresh, the app saves current findings and selected proposals in this browser’s local storage. Photo bytes are never stored. Use “Clear local plan” to remove a real plan, or clear site data in your browser.

The sample-data demo uses its own local-storage namespace. Leaving the demo does not copy its sample plan into your real workspace.

Downloads

Ledgers and bundled XMP sidecars are generated and downloaded by your browser. They do not pass through a product server.

Contact

This static product has no accounts and no contact form.
